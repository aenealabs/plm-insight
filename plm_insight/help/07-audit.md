# Audit log

Every import, decision, review, change-list export, hand-off, and refused attempt is recorded
with who did it and when.

## Searching

Search by words and filter by category: data, decisions, ECOs, exports, and sign-in and
access.

## Refused attempts

Recorded as **access denied**: someone who is not a data steward trying to decide, import, or download the change list,
someone who is not Trade Compliance trying to record a classification, approve a merge that
changes one, or request an evidence packet, and an action tried without signing in. A
sign-in that fails is recorded as **sign-in failed**.
