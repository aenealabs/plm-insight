# Scorecard

The scorecard shows data quality by business unit and rule, and how it is trending.

## Reading the scorecard

Each business unit has a quality score: the share of its parts with no open finding, as a
percentage. The table breaks open findings down by rule and severity, and the trend compares
imports over time.

## On the demo data

The demo export has known problems planted in it, so the scorecard also shows the rules
measured against them. That proves the rules work as written; real exports will be messier.
